acoustic_grand_piano-mp3.js

The acoustic grand piano from the FluidR3 GM SoundFont (FluidR3_GM.sf2,
by Frank Wen), pre-rendered to MIDI.js format by the midi-js-soundfonts
project (Benjamin Gleitzman):

  https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts

The file is redistributed unmodified. As stated by that project, the
FluidR3 GM set is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
license:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/
